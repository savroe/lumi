How It Works

Here's exactly what happens
when you hire me

No jargon, no surprises. Just a simple process that takes you from "I need a website" to "oh wow, that's actually mine."

01

We Talk

We hop on a call and you brain-dump everything! Your business, your customers, your vibe. I take notes. You don't have to know anything about websites yet.

02

The Plan

I figure out what your site actually needs (hint: it's probably not 12 pages). I'll show you the structure before any designing happens so we're on the same page.

03

The Pretty Part

This is where it starts looking like something. You'll see real designs, give feedback, and we'll go back and forth until you love it.

04

Launch Day

I build it, make sure it works on your phone, your mom's phone, and everything in between. Then i'll hand it over with a walkthrough so you actually know how to use it.

About Lumi

Design-obsessed,
results-driven

I taught myself HTML in middle school, hand coding websites about my favorite things, and never really stopped. That curiosity turned into a bachelor's degree, then a master's in Human-Centered Computing, which is really just the study of how to design technology around real people instead of the other way around. In plain terms: how to make a website that feels obvious and easy, even though there's a lot of thinking behind the scenes.

I have 8+ years of hands-on experience building sites. From clean hand-written HTML & CSS to Squarespace, Showit, and every major builder in between. I also spent a decade in the restaurant industry myself, working everything from hosting to the kitchen to management, so when I work with restaurants and hospitality brands, I'm not guessing at how the business runs, I've lived it. That's why small businesses and local shops, especially in food and hospitality, are where I do my best work.
